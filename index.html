01 OUR EDUCATIONAL MISSION

Education for
your next chapter.

You shouldn’t have to start from zero. Skills gained through military service, work and wartime necessity are a foundation for further learning.

Svitara helps Ukrainian veterans and other war-affected workers make those skills visible, identify what still needs to be learned, and build a path toward professional qualification through Ukraine’s qualification centres.

02 HOW IT WORKS

Every path is different.

A skills gap is a place to begin.

THE EDUCATIONAL PATHWAY WE’RE BUILDING

Learning with a clear purpose.

Assessment is a beginning. A partial result shows where learning can help.
Our model connects existing skills, focused education and reassessment.

01

Assess existing skills

A qualification centre assesses what you can do against the relevant professional standard.

02

Identify learning needs

Unconfirmed competencies point to the specific knowledge and practical skills to develop.

03

Learn with purpose

Focused learning addresses those gaps, with practical training in person at centres or partner workshops.

04

Return for reassessment

Further assessment gives you the opportunity to demonstrate the competencies you’ve developed.

Qualification centres make the decisions. Svitara connects the evidence and the learning pathway.

03 WHO IT’S FOR

The next chapter
starts with you.

01

Ukrainian service members & veterans

For veterans, serving personnel and other war-affected workers building on skills gained through service and work. Accessible learning and assessment are central to our model, including for people with war-related disabilities.

02

Qualification centres

A connected place to manage assessment, record competency evidence, and issue centre qualification records.

03

Vocational education partners

Our planned education model brings in training partners to help close specific knowledge and practical skill gaps, with learning grounded in professional standards.

04

Employers FUTURE PHASE

Our longer-term vision connects employers with assessed competencies. Employer access is planned for a future release.
